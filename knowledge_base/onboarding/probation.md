# Introductory Period

## Length
All new employees complete a **90-day introductory period** (sometimes called probation) from their start date.

## Introductory review
- Around **day 75**, your manager completes an introductory review in NMTech HR covering your work quality, learning and team fit.
- If the review is positive, you receive a confirmation email at day 90.
- The introductory period can be **extended once, by up to 30 days**, if more time is needed. Your manager and HR will explain why and agree clear goals with you.

## At-will employment
Employment at NMTech is **at-will** during and after the introductory period: either you or NMTech may end it at any time, with or without notice. If you decide to resign, we ask for **2 weeks' notice**.

## What applies during the introductory period
| Item | During the introductory period | After day 90 |
|---|---|---|
| Sick leave | Available from day one | Available |
| PTO | Accrues from day one, but cannot be used | Can be used |
| Medical, dental, vision, life insurance | Covered from day one | Covered |
| 401(k) and company match | From day one | From day one |
| Paid parental leave | Not yet eligible | Eligible |
| Home office stipend ($500) | Not eligible | Eligible |
| Internal job transfers | Not eligible | Eligible |

## Performance cycle
If you join on or before October 1, you take part in that year's annual review in December, even if you were recently in your introductory period.
