# Day-One Checklist for New Hires

Welcome to NMTech! Here is what to expect on your first day.

## Before you arrive
- You will receive your NMTech email address and a temporary password by text message **2 days before** your start date.
- Complete your **Form W-4** and direct-deposit details in NMTech HR using the link in your welcome email.
- Your manager and buddy will email you to introduce themselves.

## Where and when
- Report to the **lobby reception** of our Albuquerque headquarters at **8:30 AM**.
- Approved remote hires join the orientation call on Teams; the link is in your welcome email.

## Bring with you
- Original, unexpired documents for **Form I-9** employment verification – for example, a U.S. passport, **or** a driver's license plus a Social Security card. The full list of acceptable documents is on the I-9 form. Section 2 must be completed within **3 business days** of your start date.
- Bank account and routing number (or a voided check) if you have not yet set up direct deposit.

## Your first day
1. **8:30 AM** – Collect your ID badge at reception.
2. **9:00 AM** – Collect your laptop from the IT desk (3rd floor) and sign in with the temporary password. You will be asked to set a new password and set up MFA.
3. **10:00 AM** – New-hire orientation with HR (company overview, policies, benefits).
4. **12:00 PM** – Team lunch with your manager and buddy.
5. **Afternoon** – Log in to NMTech HR, check your personal details and add an emergency contact.

## Complete within your first 7 days
- MFA setup (required to keep access)
- Information Security training and Code of Conduct acknowledgement in NMTech HR

## Complete within your first 30 days
- Benefits enrollment
- Harassment-prevention training

## First 4 weeks
New hires work from the office full-time for their first 4 weeks before moving to the hybrid schedule.
