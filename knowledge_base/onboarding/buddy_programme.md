# Buddy Program

Every new hire at NMTech is paired with a **buddy** – an experienced colleague who helps you settle in.

## Who your buddy is
- Someone from your team or a closely related team, who is **not your manager**.
- Has been at NMTech for at least 1 year.
- Your buddy's name is in your welcome email; they will contact you before your first day.

## What your buddy does
- Shows you around the office and introduces you to people.
- Answers "how do things work here" questions – tools, meetings, who to ask.
- Checks in with you **weekly for the first month**, then **every two weeks until day 90**.

Your buddy is not responsible for your work goals or performance – that is your manager's role.

## Program length
The buddy program lasts **90 days** from your start date, matching your introductory period.

## Buddy lunch
Each buddy pair can claim up to **$50** for a lunch together in the first month, through NMTech HR → Expenses (category: Buddy Program).

## Feedback
You will receive short feedback surveys at **day 30** and **day 90**.

## Becoming a buddy
Employees with at least 1 year at NMTech can volunteer in NMTech HR → Programs → Buddy. Buddies receive recognition points each time they complete a program.

## Need a different buddy?
If the match is not working, contact hr-help@nmtech.com and we will reassign you, no questions asked.
