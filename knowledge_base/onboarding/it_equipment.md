# IT Equipment Allocation

## When you get your laptop
- **Office hires:** collect your laptop from the IT desk (3rd floor) on day one at 9:00 AM.
- **Approved remote hires:** your laptop is couriered to arrive **2 business days before** your start date.

## Standard laptops
| Role | Laptop |
|---|---|
| Engineering, Data and Design | Apple MacBook Pro 14" (M-series, 32 GB RAM) |
| All other roles | Dell Latitude 7450 (Intel Core Ultra 7, 16 GB RAM) |

Every laptop comes with a charger and a laptop bag. Laptops are replaced every **3 years**.

## Accessories
On request through the IT Service Portal → **Request Hardware**:
- One external monitor (24") for home use
- Headset with microphone
- Keyboard and mouse

Accessory requests are fulfilled within **5 business days**. Office desks already have a monitor, keyboard and mouse.

## Looking after your equipment
- Equipment remains NMTech property. Do not install unapproved software or let others use your laptop.
- Report a lost or stolen laptop **immediately** by calling ext. 2020 – it is treated as a Critical (P1) security incident.
- Accidental damage is repaired by IT at no cost the first time. Repeated damage due to negligence may be recovered from the employee.

## Leaving NMTech
Return your laptop and accessories to the IT desk on your last working day, or courier them using the prepaid label IT sends you.
