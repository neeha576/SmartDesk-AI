# Benefits Enrollment

Your NMTech benefits start **on your first day of employment**, including during your introductory period.

## What you get
| Benefit | Coverage | Cost to you |
|---|---|---|
| Medical | Choice of a PPO plan or a High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with HSA | NMTech pays 90% of the employee premium and 75% of dependent premiums |
| Health Savings Account (HSA) | NMTech contributes $1,000 per year (employee only) or $2,000 per year (family) if you choose the HDHP | Free |
| Dental and vision | Preventive care covered at 100% | NMTech pays 90% of the employee premium |
| Basic life and AD&D insurance | 2 × your base salary, up to $500,000 | Free |
| Short- and long-term disability | 60% of base salary while you are unable to work | Free |
| 401(k) retirement plan | NMTech matches 100% of the first 4% of base salary you contribute; the match vests immediately | Your contribution |
| Employee Assistance Program (EAP) | Free, confidential counseling for you and your household | Free |

## Who you can cover
Your spouse or registered domestic partner, and children up to **age 26**.

## How to enroll
1. Go to **NMTech HR → Benefits → Enroll**.
2. Choose your medical plan (PPO or HDHP), and dental and vision coverage.
3. Add dependents with their dates of birth.
4. Set your 401(k) contribution percentage (you can change it at any time).
5. Submit. Insurance ID cards arrive by mail within **10 business days**; digital cards are available sooner in the insurer's app.

You must enroll within **30 days of your start date**. If you miss the window, you are enrolled in employee-only PPO coverage and can make changes at the next open enrollment.

## Open enrollment
Each **November** you can switch plans and add or remove dependents for the plan year starting **January 1**.

## Qualifying life events
If you marry, have or adopt a child, or a dependent loses other coverage, update your benefits in NMTech HR within **30 days** of the event.
