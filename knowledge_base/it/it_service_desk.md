# IT Service Desk – Contacts, Hours and Priorities

## How to reach IT
- **IT Service Portal:** https://it.nmtech.com (preferred for all non-urgent requests)
- **Phone:** ext. 2020
- **Email:** it-helpdesk@nmtech.com
- **Walk-in:** IT desk, 3rd floor, Albuquerque headquarters

## Hours
- Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM Mountain Time (MT).
- For **Critical (P1)** issues outside hours, call ext. 2020 and choose option 9 to reach the on-call engineer.

## Priority levels and response targets
| Priority | Example | First response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical (P1) | Company-wide outage, security incident, lost laptop | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| High (P2) | You cannot work at all (laptop won't boot, locked out) | 1 hour | 1 business day |
| Medium (P3) | Something is broken but there is a workaround | 4 business hours | 3 business days |
| Low (P4) | Requests and questions | 1 business day | 5 business days |

## Ticket statuses
Open → In Progress → Waiting on Employee → Resolved → Closed. A Resolved ticket closes automatically after 3 business days if you do not reply.
