# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Setup

MFA is **mandatory** for all NMTech accounts. It is required for email, VPN, NovaHR, the IT Service Portal and the Self-Service Password Portal.

## Approved method
- **Microsoft Authenticator** app (push notification or 6-digit code) – primary method.
- **SMS to your registered mobile** – backup method only.
- Hardware security keys are issued only to administrators and finance approvers.

## First-time setup (complete on day one)
1. Install **Microsoft Authenticator** from the App Store or Google Play on your phone.
2. On your laptop, go to https://aka.ms/mfasetup and sign in with your NMTech email.
3. Click **Add sign-in method → Authenticator app** and scan the QR code with the app.
4. Approve the test notification.
5. Add your mobile number as a backup SMS method.

You have **7 days from joining** to complete setup. After that, sign-in is blocked until IT helps you enroll.

## Moving to a new phone
1. Before you reset or give away your old phone, install Authenticator on the new phone.
2. Go to https://aka.ms/mfasetup, add the new device, then delete the old one.

## Lost or stolen phone
Call the IT Service Desk on ext. 2020 immediately. After verifying your identity, IT will remove the old device and issue a **Temporary Access Pass valid for 8 hours** so you can enroll a new phone.

## Stop "MFA fatigue" attacks
If you get an MFA prompt you did not start, **deny it** and report it to security@nmtech.com. Never approve a prompt because someone asked you to.
