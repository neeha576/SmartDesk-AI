# Email on Mobile Devices

You can read NMTech email, calendar and Teams on your personal or company phone, as long as you use the approved apps.

## Requirements
- **Microsoft Outlook** app only. The built-in iPhone Mail app and Gmail app are blocked for company mail.
- iOS 16 or later, or Android 12 or later.
- MFA must already be set up.
- A device PIN, fingerprint or face unlock must be enabled.

## Setup steps
1. Install **Microsoft Outlook** from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Open Outlook and enter your NMTech email address.
3. Sign in with your password and approve the MFA prompt.
4. When asked, allow NMTech to protect data in the app. Outlook will ask you to set a **6-digit app PIN**.
5. Install **Microsoft Teams** the same way if you need chat on mobile.

You do **not** need to enroll your whole phone in device management. NMTech only manages company data inside Outlook and Teams.

## What NMTech can and cannot see
- **Can:** remove company email and files from the apps, require an app PIN, block copy-paste from Outlook into personal apps.
- **Cannot:** see your personal photos, messages, browsing or location.

## Lost or stolen phone
Report it within **24 hours** by calling ext. 2020. IT will remotely wipe company data from Outlook and Teams. Your personal data is not touched.
