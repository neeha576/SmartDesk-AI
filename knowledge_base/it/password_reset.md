# Password Reset and Account Lockout

Your NMTech account (one sign-on) is used for your laptop, email, VPN, NovaHR and the IT Service Portal. Changing the password changes it everywhere.

## Password rules
- Minimum 12 characters, with at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number and one symbol.
- Passwords expire every 90 days. You get reminder emails 14, 7 and 1 day before expiry.
- You cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords.
- Do not use your name, employee ID or "NMTech" in the password.

## Resetting a forgotten password (self-service)
1. Go to the Self-Service Password Portal at https://passwords.nmtech.com.
2. Click **Forgot Password** and enter your employee ID.
3. Approve the request in Microsoft Authenticator, or enter the code sent by SMS to your registered mobile number.
4. Choose a new password that meets the rules above.
5. If you are on a company laptop away from the office, connect to the VPN once after resetting so the laptop picks up the new password.

Self-service reset only works if you have already set up MFA (see *MFA Setup*).

## Changing a password you still know
On Windows press **Ctrl+Alt+Del → Change a password**. On a Mac, use **System Settings → Users & Groups → Change Password** while connected to the office network or VPN.

## Account lockout
- Your account locks after **5 failed sign-in attempts**.
- It unlocks automatically after **30 minutes**.
- If you cannot wait, call the IT Service Desk on **ext. 2020** with your employee ID. The agent will verify your identity with a code sent to your registered mobile before unlocking.

## Security reminders
IT will never ask for your password by phone, chat or email. Report suspicious requests to security@nmtech.com.
