# Time Off and Leave Policy

The time-off year runs from **January 1 to December 31**. All time off is requested and approved in **NMTech HR → Time Off**. Allowances for employees who join or leave mid-year are prorated by month. One day equals 8 hours.

## Time off at a glance
| Type | Allowance per year | Carryover | Paid out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paid time off (PTO) | 15 days (120 hours) | Up to 5 days (40 hours) | Up to 5 days cash-out in December; full balance paid when you leave |
| Sick leave | 8 days (64 hours) | Up to 8 days (64 hours) | No |
| Floating holidays | 2 days | No | No |
| Company holidays | 10 days | – | – |
| Bereavement leave | Up to 5 days per event | – | – |
| Paid parental leave (maternity / paternity) | 12 weeks (birthing parent) / 6 weeks (non-birthing or adoptive parent) | – | – |

The company holiday calendar is published in NMTech HR every December.

## Paid time off (PTO)
- Used for vacation and personal matters.
- Accrues at **1.25 days (10 hours) per month** from your first day.
- Can be used once you complete your **90-day introductory period**.
- Request PTO at least **2 weeks in advance** for absences longer than 3 days, and at least **2 days in advance** otherwise.
- You can carry over up to **5 days** into the next year; anything above 5 days is forfeited on December 31.
- Each December you may cash out up to **5 unused PTO days**, paid at your base rate in the January paycheck.
- Accrued, unused PTO is paid out in your final paycheck when you leave NMTech.

## Sick leave
- **8 days (64 hours) per year**, available from your first day. This meets the New Mexico Healthy Workplaces Act.
- Use it for your own illness or medical care, or to care for a family member.
- Tell your manager before your start time on the day you are unwell, and record it in NMTech HR when you are back.
- A doctor's note is required for **more than 3 consecutive days** of sick leave.
- Unused sick leave carries over up to **8 days (64 hours)**. Sick leave is **not paid out**, either at year end or when you leave.

## Floating holidays
2 floating holidays per year to use on days that matter to you. They do not carry over.

## Paid parental leave (maternity, paternity and adoption leave)
- **Birthing parent (maternity leave):** 12 weeks of fully paid leave.
- **Non-birthing parent (paternity leave) or adoptive parent:** 6 weeks of fully paid leave.
- Eligible after **90 days** of employment. Leave must be taken within **12 months** of the birth or placement and can be taken in up to two blocks.
- For eligible employees, parental leave runs at the same time as federal FMLA leave.

## Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)
Employees with at least 12 months of service and 1,250 hours worked in the past year may take up to **12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave** for qualifying family and medical reasons. Contact hr-help@nmtech.com to start a request.

## Working on a company holiday
- **Non-exempt (hourly) employees** are paid 1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked on a company holiday.
- **Exempt (salaried) employees** asked by their manager to work on a company holiday receive **1 substitute day off**, to be used within **60 days**.

## Unpaid time off
If you have no PTO left, your manager may approve unpaid time off, which is deducted from your pay.
