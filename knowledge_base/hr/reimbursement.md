# Expense Reimbursement Process

Business expenses you pay for personally are reimbursed through **NMTech HR → Expenses**.

## How to submit a claim
1. Go to **NMTech HR → Expenses → New Claim**.
2. Choose the expense category and enter the date, amount and business purpose.
3. Upload a clear photo or PDF of the receipt. Receipts are **required for any item over $25**.
4. Submit. Your manager approves first, then Finance reviews.

## Deadlines and payment
- Submit claims within **30 days** of the expense date. Claims older than 30 days are rejected unless your department head approves the delay.
- Approved claims are paid by **direct deposit with the next paycheck**, as long as Finance approves them at least **5 business days before payday**. Later approvals are paid on the following payday.
- Track the status (Submitted → Manager Approved → Finance Approved → Paid) in NMTech HR.
- Reimbursements are not taxable income and are shown separately on your pay stub.

## Reimbursable categories and limits
| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Internet (remote work) | $50 per month |
| Client meals | $75 per person per meal; list attendees |
| Local rideshare or taxi for work trips | Actual cost, with receipt |
| Personal car used for work | Current IRS standard mileage rate |
| Parking at client sites | Actual cost, with receipt |
| Learning and certifications | $2,000 per year, pre-approved by your manager |
| Home office (one-time, after introductory period) | $500 |

## Not reimbursable
Your daily commute to the office, traffic or parking fines, alcohol (except client entertainment approved in advance by a director), personal entertainment, and expenses already paid by a client.

## Rejected claims
If Finance rejects a claim, the reason appears in NMTech HR. Fix it and resubmit within 10 days.
