# Code of Conduct

The Code of Conduct applies to every NMTech employee, intern and contractor. You must read and acknowledge it in NMTech HR **within 7 days of joining** and again every January.

## Respect at work
Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Discrimination, bullying and harassment of any kind are not tolerated (see *Anti-Harassment and Non-Discrimination Policy*).

## Conflicts of interest
Disclose any situation where your personal interests could affect your work for NMTech – for example, a family member working at a vendor, or an investment in a competitor. Disclose through **NMTech HR → Compliance → Conflict of Interest**.

## Gifts and entertainment
- You may accept gifts worth up to **$100** per person per year from any single business partner.
- Anything above that must be declined or reported to Compliance.
- Cash, gift cards and vouchers may never be accepted, whatever the amount.

## Outside employment
Second jobs, freelancing or paid board roles require written approval from your manager and HR before you start. Work that competes with NMTech is not permitted.

## Confidential information
Customer data, source code, financial results and product plans are confidential. Do not share them outside NMTech or store them on personal devices or personal cloud accounts.

## Social media
You may say you work at NMTech, but do not speak on the company's behalf, share confidential information, or post about customers without approval from Marketing. Nothing in this Code limits your legal right to discuss your wages or working conditions.

## Reporting concerns
- Talk to your manager or HR, or
- Email ethics@nmtech.com, or
- Use the anonymous **Ethics Hotline** (link in NMTech HR).

NMTech does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Consequences
Breaches of the Code can lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
