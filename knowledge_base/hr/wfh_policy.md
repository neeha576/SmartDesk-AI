# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

NMTech follows a **hybrid model**. Employees near our Albuquerque headquarters work from the office **3 days a week** and from home **2 days a week**.

## Office days
- **Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday** are anchor days when everyone is expected in the office.
- Teams may agree a different pattern with their department head, but it must still include 3 office days.
- New hires work from the office full-time for their **first 4 weeks**.

## Core hours
Everyone should be available between **10:00 AM and 3:00 PM Mountain Time (MT)**, whether at home or in the office. Outside core hours you may set your own schedule with your manager's agreement.

## Fully remote work
Fully remote arrangements are approved only by exception (for example, medical or caregiving needs). They need approval from your manager, your VP and HR, and are reviewed every 6 months.

## Working from another state
You may work temporarily from another U.S. state for up to **30 days per calendar year** with your manager's approval. Record it in NMTech HR → Work Location before you go, because payroll may need to withhold that state's taxes.

Working from **outside the United States** is not allowed without written approval from HR and Legal, because of tax, immigration and data-protection rules.

## Home setup and stipends
- **Internet stipend:** $50 per month, claimed through the expense process (see *Expense Reimbursement Process*).
- **One-time home office stipend:** up to $500 for a chair, desk or other ergonomic equipment, claimable after your 90-day introductory period.
- You must connect to the VPN for internal systems and lock your screen when away.
- Do not work on confidential data in public places such as coffee shops or co-working spaces.

## Availability
On remote days keep your Teams status up to date, turn your camera on for team meetings where possible, and respond to messages within 1 hour during core hours.
