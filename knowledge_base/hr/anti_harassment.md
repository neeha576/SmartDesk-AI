# Anti-Harassment and Non-Discrimination Policy

NMTech prohibits harassment and discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, pregnancy, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, ancestry, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, or any other status protected by federal law or the New Mexico Human Rights Act. This policy protects **employees of every gender and background**.

## What counts as harassment
Unwelcome conduct based on a protected characteristic that is severe or frequent enough to create an intimidating, hostile or offensive work environment. **Sexual harassment** includes unwelcome physical contact, requests for sexual favors, sexual comments or jokes, and sharing sexual images. The policy applies in the office, at off-sites, at client sites, during work travel and in online channels such as Teams or text-message work groups.

## How to report
You can report to any of these – you do **not** have to go through your manager:
- Your manager or any other manager
- People Operations at **hr-help@nmtech.com**
- The confidential **Employee Relations line** at **conduct@nmtech.com**
- The anonymous **Ethics Hotline** (link in NMTech HR)

Report as soon as possible so we can act quickly. Managers who receive a report must pass it to People Operations within **1 business day**.

## What happens next
- People Operations acknowledges your report within **2 business days**.
- A trained investigator interviews the people involved and reviews evidence.
- Most investigations are completed within **30 days**. You are told when it is closed and, where appropriate, what action was taken.
- While the investigation is open, NMTech may take interim steps, such as changing reporting lines or schedules.

## Confidentiality and protection from retaliation
Information is shared only with those who need it to investigate and resolve the concern. Retaliation against anyone who reports or takes part in an investigation is itself a violation of this policy.

## Outside agencies
You also have the right to contact the U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC) or the New Mexico Human Rights Bureau.

## Mandatory training
All employees complete harassment-prevention training **within 30 days of joining** and once every year.
