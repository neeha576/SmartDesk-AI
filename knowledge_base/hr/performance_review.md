# Performance Review Cycle

NMTech's performance year follows the **calendar year (January 1 – December 31)**.

## Timeline
| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| January | Goals set for the new year in NMTech HR → Performance |
| July | Mid-year check-in with your manager (no rating) |
| December | Self-assessment, peer feedback and manager assessment |
| January | Calibration across teams |
| February | Final ratings shared in review conversations |
| March 1 | Merit increases and promotions take effect (annual bonus paid by March 15) |

## Steps in the annual review
1. **Self-assessment** – rate yourself against your goals and add examples.
2. **Peer feedback** – choose 2 to 4 colleagues to give feedback; your manager may add more.
3. **Manager assessment** – your manager rates you and writes comments.
4. **Calibration** – department leaders review ratings together for fairness.
5. **Review conversation** – your manager discusses your rating and next year's goals with you.

## Rating scale
1 – Below expectations · 2 – Partially meets · 3 – Meets expectations · 4 – Exceeds expectations · 5 – Outstanding.

## Who is eligible
Employees who joined **on or before October 1** take part in that year's cycle. Later hires are reviewed in the following cycle.

## Promotions
Promotion cycles run twice a year, effective **March 1 and September 1**. Your manager nominates you; nominations are reviewed by the department head and HR.

## Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
An employee rated 1 may be placed on a **60-day** PIP with clear goals and check-ins every two weeks with their manager and HR.

## Bonus link
Your individual rating affects your annual bonus (see *Compensation Structure*).
