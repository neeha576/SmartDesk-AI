# Stock Options and Vesting

Eligible roles receive stock options under the **NMTech 2023 Equity Incentive Plan**. Your grant notice states the number of options, the type (ISO or NSO) and the exercise (strike) price.

## Vesting schedule
- **Total vesting period:** 4 years from the vesting start date.
- **1-year cliff:** nothing vests in the first 12 months. On the first anniversary, **25%** of your options vest.
- **After the cliff:** the remaining 75% vests **quarterly** at **6.25%** per quarter over the next 3 years.

**Example:** with 4,000 options and a vesting start date of July 1, 2026, 1,000 vest on July 1, 2027, then 250 vest every quarter until all 4,000 have vested on July 1, 2030.

## Where to see your options
Log in to the **EquityHub** portal (link in NMTech HR → Pay → Stock Options) to see your grants, vested and unvested options, and to accept new grants.

## Accepting a grant
Accept your grant in EquityHub within **60 days** of receiving it. Grants not accepted within 60 days are cancelled.

## Exercising options
- You can exercise vested options at any time while employed, up to **10 years** from the grant date.
- To exercise, submit a request in EquityHub and pay the exercise price.
- **Incentive stock options (ISOs):** generally no regular income tax when you exercise, but the gain may count toward the alternative minimum tax (AMT).
- **Non-qualified stock options (NSOs):** the difference between the fair market value and the exercise price is taxed as ordinary income when you exercise and appears on your W-2.
- Consult your own tax advisor before exercising.

## If you leave NMTech
- **Unvested** options are cancelled on your last day of employment.
- **Vested** options must be exercised within **90 days** of your last day, or they are cancelled.
