# Payday Schedule

## When you are paid
- NMTech pays **every other Friday (biweekly)** – **26 paychecks** a year.
- Each pay period covers two weeks, Sunday through Saturday. Payday is the Friday after the pay period ends.
- If payday falls on a bank holiday, you are paid on the business day before.
- Pay is made by **direct deposit**. Pay stubs are available in **NMTech HR → Pay → Pay Stubs** on payday.

## Timesheets (hourly employees)
Non-exempt (hourly) employees must submit and get approval for timesheets by **10:00 AM MT on the Monday** after the pay period ends. Late timesheets are paid on the next payday. Overtime is paid at **1.5 times** your regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek.

## Your first paycheck
You are paid on the first payday after your first pay period ends – usually **1 to 3 weeks** after your start date, depending on where your start date falls in the pay period.

## Changing your direct deposit
Update your bank details in **NMTech HR → Profile → Direct Deposit** at least **5 business days before payday** for the change to apply to that paycheck. You can split pay across up to 3 accounts.

## What appears on your pay stub
Earnings (base pay, overtime, bonuses and expense reimbursements), pre-tax deductions (401(k), medical, dental, vision, HSA), taxes (federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare and New Mexico state income tax), and net pay.

## Questions or errors
Email payroll@nmtech.com. Errors in your favor or against you are corrected on the next payday.
