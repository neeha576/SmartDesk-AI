# Compensation Structure

Your offer letter states your **annual base salary** (or hourly rate), your **target bonus**, and any stock options.

## Components
| Component | How it works |
|---|---|
| Base pay | Annual salary for exempt employees; hourly rate for non-exempt employees, paid biweekly |
| Overtime | Non-exempt employees only: 1.5 times the hourly rate for hours over 40 in a workweek |
| Annual bonus | Target of 10% of base salary for most roles; sales roles have a separate commission plan |
| 401(k) match | NMTech matches 100% of the first 4% of base salary you contribute; vests immediately |
| Stock options | Granted to eligible roles (see *Stock Options and Vesting*) |
| Benefits | Medical, dental, vision, life, disability and HSA contributions (see *Benefits Enrollment*) |

## Deductions from your paycheck
- **Federal income tax** – based on your W-4
- **Social Security** (6.2%) and **Medicare** (1.45%)
- **New Mexico state income tax**
- **Pre-tax deductions:** 401(k), medical, dental and vision premiums, HSA

## Annual bonus
- Paid once a year by **March 15**, for the previous January–December performance year.
- The payout depends on company performance and your individual rating:

| Rating | Payout of your target bonus |
|---|---|
| 5 – Outstanding | 150% |
| 4 – Exceeds | 120% |
| 3 – Meets | 100% |
| 2 – Partially meets | 50% |
| 1 – Below | 0% |

- **Example:** with a $90,000 base salary, the target bonus is $9,000. A rating of 4 pays $10,800.
- To be eligible, you must have joined **on or before October 1** of the performance year and be employed on the payout date.

## Merit increases
Annual merit increases are based on your performance rating and take effect on **March 1**. Your updated compensation letter is available in NMTech HR in February.
