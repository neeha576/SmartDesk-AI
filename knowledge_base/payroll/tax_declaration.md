# Tax Withholding (W-4) and W-2 Forms

NMTech withholds federal and state income tax from every paycheck. The amount depends on the elections on your Form W-4.

## Form W-4 (federal withholding)
- Complete your **W-4** in **NMTech HR → Pay → Tax Forms** before your first payday.
- If you do not submit a W-4, payroll withholds as if you were **single with no adjustments**, as IRS rules require.
- You can update your W-4 **at any time** – for example after marriage, a new child or a second job. Changes apply from the next payday if submitted at least **5 business days before payday**.
- Use the IRS Tax Withholding Estimator on irs.gov if you are unsure what to enter.

## State income tax
- **New Mexico** income tax is withheld based on your W-4 elections.
- If you work from another state (see *Remote and Hybrid Work Policy*), record it in NMTech HR → Work Location so payroll can withhold for the correct state.

## Other taxes on your paycheck
- **Social Security:** 6.2% of wages
- **Medicare:** 1.45% of wages
- 401(k), medical, dental, vision and HSA contributions are taken **before** income tax, which lowers your taxable wages.

## Form W-2
- Your **W-2** for the previous year is available in NMTech HR by **January 31**.
- If you consented to electronic delivery, you can download it from NMTech HR; otherwise a paper copy is mailed to your home address on file.
- Former employees can request a copy at payroll@nmtech.com.

## Update your address
Keep your home address current in NMTech HR so your W-2 and state withholding are correct.

NMTech payroll cannot give personal tax advice. For questions about the process, email payroll@nmtech.com.
