# Deliberate Knowledge-Base Gaps

These topics are **intentionally not covered** anywhere in the knowledge base. Questions about them
must trigger the escalation path (offer to create a ticket) instead of an answer.
Do not add content on these topics, or the escalation tests stop working.

## IT gaps (escalate to an IT ticket)
| Test question | Why it's a gap |
|---|---|
| My monitor has been flickering for two days. | Hardware troubleshooting is not documented |
| How do I add the office printer to my laptop? | No printer setup guide exists |
| My laptop battery drains in an hour. | No laptop hardware diagnostics |
| My Bluetooth headset won't pair with my laptop. | No peripheral troubleshooting |
| How do I book a meeting room? | Room booking is not documented |
| Jira is showing a 500 error. | Application outages need a human |

## HR gaps (escalate to an HR ticket)
| Test question | Why it's a gap |
|---|---|
| Where can I park at the office? | Parking policy not documented |
| Is there a relocation allowance for moving to Albuquerque? | Relocation not covered |
| Can I take a 3-month sabbatical? | Sabbatical not covered |
| How much is the employee referral bonus? | Referral program not documented |

| Does NMTech pay for a gym membership? | Wellness reimbursements not covered |
| What's on the cafeteria menu this week? | Cafeteria not documented |
